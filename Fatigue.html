Fatigue

It's time to talk about the fatigue

The fatigue is defined as extreme tiredness that makes it difficult to carry out simple daily tasks.

This can be caused by different factors, both emotional and physical.

Let's explore some causes

What causes fatigue?
Lack of rest

Not sleeping enough reduces both physical and mental energy.

Constant stress

When the mind never rests, the body feels exhausted.

Mental overload

Thinking too much can be as tiring as physical effort.

Too much screen time

Constant stimulation drains your focus and energy.
